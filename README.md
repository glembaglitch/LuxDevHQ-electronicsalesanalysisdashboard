# 📊 Electronics Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **Electronics Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor and analyze the sales performance of an electronics business. It provides decision-makers with real-time insights into revenue, profit, discounts, product performance, and geographical sales distribution.

The dashboard enables users to identify high-performing cities, profitable product categories, and top-selling products while allowing dynamic filtering through slicers.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance using key sales metrics.
- Compare sales and profit across different cities.
- Analyze profit contribution by product category.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Enable interactive data exploration through filters.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays the following business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Total Revenue** | Total revenue generated from all sales. |
| **Total Profit** | Overall profit earned from sales. |
| **Average Profit** | Average profit per transaction. |
| **Average Discount** | Average discount offered across all products. |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Total Sales Amount by City
- Horizontal bar chart
- Compares total sales generated across different cities.
- Helps identify the highest and lowest performing locations.

### 2. Total Profit by Product Category
- Pie Chart
- Displays the percentage contribution of each product category to total profit.
- Categories include:
  - Computers
  - Home Appliances
  - Electronics

### 3. Total Sales by Product Category
- Column Chart
- Compares sales performance across product categories.

### 4. Total Sales by Product
- Column Chart
- Highlights the best-selling products based on total sales.

### 5. Interactive Filters (Slicers)
Users can filter dashboard results by:

- City
- Product Category

All visuals update dynamically based on the selected filters.
